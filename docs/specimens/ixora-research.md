## Source-backed traits

[Kew accepts _Ixora coccinea_ L.](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A753844-1).
The authority matters: _Ixora coccinea_ Curtis is a synonym of _I. chinensis_,
not the taxon selected here.

[Flora of Pakistan](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=5&taxon_id=220006906)
describes a branched shrub with opposite decussate, mostly sessile, entire leaves;
cuspidate or awned triangular stipules; and terminal dense corymbiform cymes.
Sessile flowers have a slender corolla tube 25–40 mm long and about 1.5 mm wide,
four lobes twisted in bud, a glabrous throat, four very short-filamented stamens
at the throat, and an exserted style. Anthers shed early and release pollen just
after opening while the stigma is immature. This is evidence for reproductive
phase differences, not a measured animation schedule.

[The Taiwan flora's Ixora account](https://tai2.ntu.edu.tw/family/Rubiaceae/Ixora)
provides genus-level support for four calyx teeth, mouth-mounted stamens, a
filiform style and two linear stigma lobes.
[NC State Extension](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ixora-coccinea/)
documents smooth reddish woody stems and glossy, leathery, hairless elliptical
to oblong leaves. Its species description distinguishes four-lobed flowers from
the broader website category fields.
