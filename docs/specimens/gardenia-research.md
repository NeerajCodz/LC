# Gardenia — Gardenia jasminoides

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [Flora of China](https://www.iplant.cn/foc/pdf/Gardenia.pdf) distinguishes the long corolla tube and prominent calyx lobes of G. jasminoides. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/gardenia-jasminoides/) records fragrant white flowers, glossy evergreen leaves and single/double variation. This study uses a single form.

Authored composition: one six-lobed ivory flower and two buds, a continuous long corolla tube, six included anthers, a central style and inferior ovary. Opposite leathery leaves and interpetiolar stipules attach to a woody shoot.

Surface: waxy ivory petals, subtle cream throat, glossy dark foliage and visible branching veins. Twisted buds expand while preserving the fused throat. The woody carrier is restrained; distal lobes yield under focused interaction. Counts, dimensions, palette and mechanics are authored. Cultivar identity, scent, nectar, exact epidermal anatomy and measured response remain gaps.
