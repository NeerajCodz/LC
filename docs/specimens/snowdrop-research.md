# Snowdrop — Galanthus nivalis

Reviewed October 7, 2026. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/galanthus-nivalis/)
describes a solitary flower per scape, a green terminal bract, three long outer
tepals, three shorter notched green-marked inner segments and narrow basal leaves.
[Primary floral-anatomy research](https://doi.org/10.5586/asbp.3486) supplies
reproductive anatomy including six stamens and the inferior ovary. The paper's
indexed text was available; the DOI landing page could not be fetched locally.

Author a clump of two mature scapes and one bud scape, each with one flower and
two leaves. Keep unequal tepal whorls, separate ovary, curved pedicel and spathe.
Ivory tissue, inner apical green markings, blue-green leaves, proportions and
firm inner/softer outer tepal responses are authored. Enclose organs in the
inner segments throughout reverse bloom.

This clump does not imply several flowers on one scape. Temperature-dependent
opening, bulb growth, measured pigments and mechanics are not predicted.
