# Chinese Wisteria — Wisteria sinensis

Research reviewed 12 October 2026. This dossier guides a dedicated authored 3D
study; inventory mapping and visual plausibility do not establish scientific validation.

## Source-backed traits

A counter-clockwise twining woody vine has pinnate leaves, usually eleven leaflets, and hanging blue-violet racemes. Flowers in one raceme tend to open together before full leaf expansion.

[Primary reference](https://landscapeplants.oregonstate.edu/plants/wisteria-sinensis)
[Additional reference](https://www.iplant.cn/foc/pdf/Wisteria.pdf).

## Authored implementation

Sixteen bilateral pea flowers, each with a banner, two wings and paired keel, ten enclosed stamens and a style, on one pendant rachis.

Foliage: Eleven leaflets per compound leaf; a woody shoot wraps a fixed support without tendrils.

Behavior: Supported shoot, independent pendant flowers, and coupled wing/keel depression with elastic recovery. Bloom can be reversed for inspection; this is not a
claim of biological reopening. Contact, stiffness, damping, colors, counts of
flowers/leaves, dimensions and stage timing are authored. Soft tissue uses sealed
parametric shells with separate folded positions/normals; fine reproductive organs
retain real geometry. Shared atlas sampling supplies cell/pigment irregularity,
with restrained scatter and distinct tissue roughness. Focused/selected physics
uses the existing 2,048/512-node budget; distant views remain ambient.

## Evidence gaps

Leaf expansion and synchronized opening are an authored display, not a phenology simulation. Papilionoid organ counts need the Flora of China genus treatment alongside the habit source. No measured elastic constants, spectral pigment data or tissue scans are
available. Full seasonal growth, pollinator action and fertilization are outside
this study. Review front, side, 45 degrees, macro, closed bud and half bloom before
calling the specimen finished.
