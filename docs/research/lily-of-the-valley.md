# Lily of the Valley — Convallaria majalis

Reviewed October 7, 2026. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/convallaria-majalis/)
documents pendant white bells and broad basal foliage.
[Flora of China](https://www.iplant.cn/foc/pdf/Convallaria.pdf) provides the
raceme, six perianth lobes and six included stamens.

Author eight flowers and three younger buds on an arching raceme with two broad
leaves. Continuous swollen bell walls terminate in six turned-back teeth.
Included stamens/style, pedicel attachments, narrow bud mouths and parallel
leaf veins remain visible in macro. White waxiness, lengths, age offsets and
independent bell nodding are authored; rims deform more gently than blades.

Rhizome spread, fragrance, fruiting, reopening and calibrated material response
are outside the simulation. The two leaves are a composition choice rather
than a universal specimen count.
