# Foxglove — Digitalis purpurea

Sources reviewed October 4, 2026. Model a native-type purple, one-sided raceme;
avoid peloric terminal flowers and all-around hybrid arrangements.

[NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/digitalis-purpurea/) describes
pendulous inflated corolla tubes, shorter upper lips, spotted pale interiors,
interior hairs, downy rugose leaves and a basal rosette below smaller stem leaves.
[Royal Botanic Gardens Sydney](https://plantnet.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/cgi-bin/NSWfl.pl?lvl=gn&name=Digitalis&page=nswfl)
documents four stamens in two pairs and bilabiate corollas.
[Mackin et al.](https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1365-2745.13636)
document bottom-to-top opening and the separation of male/female phases.

Author twelve complete bells and younger terminal buds. Each has a sealed,
continuous hollow wall, calyx, four included stamens and a central style, with
maculation restricted to the throat. Preserve velvety leaves, rounded teeth,
prominent veins and quality-scaled hairs. Opening is staggered upward; upper buds
remain younger in the mature composition. Individual pedicels nod while stems
and foliage share the rooted bend. Numeric mechanics and pigment fields are
authored; no pollinator agent, reproductive timing or species-identification claim
is implied by the reversible animation.
