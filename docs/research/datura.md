# Devil’s Trumpet — Datura stramonium

Reviewed 2 November 2026. Taxon: *Datura stramonium*, Solanaceae. [Implementation guide](../specimens/datura.md).

## Evidence and reference proportions

[Burke Herbarium / Flora of North America](https://burkeherbarium.org/imagecollection/taxon.php?Taxon=Datura+stramonium) describes a narrow 6–10 cm funnel, 3–5 cm across its limb, five projecting teeth, a cylindrical five-toothed calyx and five included stamens attached within the corolla. Flowers are terminal but appear in mature branch forks. Its leaves have a few coarse pointed teeth. [NC State Extension](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/datura-stramonium/) supports the upright branching habit, alternate simple foliage and white, sometimes purple-tinged flowers that open at night and wilt the following day.

This study represents the upright narrow trumpet of this taxon. A broad pendent Brugmansia bell would convey a different plant.

## Authored study

One continuous hollow five-fold corolla, one tubular calyx, five included filaments/anthers and a central style with stigma lobes sit above a forked shoot. The display trumpet is about 1.5 units long above a 1.65-unit main stem. Four coarsely toothed leaves use continuous relief geometry and attached petioles. Their fork-tip attachments follow the same growth and clamped wind curve as the custom branches.

Opening unfurls the pleats through corresponding sealed open/folded skins and normals. The exterior skin is identified geometrically, with green basal shading and subtle pleat highlights. Roughness 0.66, scattering 0.065 and 220 / 70 contact nodes are authored. Pointer and wind response use the common bounded solver; the included organs remain attached throughout the morph.

## Evidence gaps and verification

Reverse bloom does not represent biological reopening of a spent flower. No circadian model, scent transport, seed capsule or pharmacology is simulated. Scale, leaf count, wall thickness, pigments and mechanics are display parameters. Interior clearance tests sample the actual generated geometry; they do not prove every possible deformed configuration collision-free.
