# Great White Trillium — Trillium grandiflorum

Reviewed 2 November 2026. Taxon: *Trillium grandiflorum*, Melanthiaceae. [Implementation guide](../specimens/trillium.md).

## Evidence and reference proportions

[NC State Extension](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/trillium-grandiflorum/) describes a woodland perennial with a large white flower held above three broad green bracts; flowers are about 2–4 inches across. The bracts resemble leaves but belong at a single terminal level, above an otherwise bare scape. [Chicago Botanic Garden](https://www.chicagobotanic.org/plant-information/plant-profiles/great-white-trillium) provides a second species reference and photographs. The Northern Ontario flora description was reviewed during initial research; a later access attempt timed out: [source](https://northernontarioflora.ca/?m=I1&sd=1002574).

Preserve the three-petalled plan, three alternating sepals, six stamens and central angular ovary with three stigma branches. White petals are broad and undulate rather than narrow triangular planes. Green bracts have conspicuous longitudinal veins and sessile insertions.

## Authored study

One flower, three bracts at the same insertion, scape length 1.4 scene units, petal length about 0.9 and bract length 1.45 are display choices, not a metric reconstruction. Petals have sealed 0.012-unit walls, subtly rolled margins and a blunt finite apex. Contact grids retain 270 desktop / 90 constrained nodes. The apex accommodates their finite contact thickness instead of collapsing several nonadjacent nodes into one point.

White/ivory pigment, restrained branching veins, roughness 0.56 and scattering 0.055 are authored optical parameters. Fine relief uses the shared tissue atlas and procedural fallback. Opening raises and spreads the same three petal shells; attachments stay fixed while wind and local contact deform their distal tissue.

## Evidence gaps and verification

The reversible bloom slider is a display morph. It does not model seasonal pink ageing, scent, rhizomes, pollination, fruiting or measured tissue mechanics. Contact constants, curvature and dimensions are uncalibrated.
