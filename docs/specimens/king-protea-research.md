# King Protea — Protea cynaroides

Sources reviewed October 6, 2026. Depict one substantial flower head on a woody
shoot, not a single huge radial flower or a reconstructed mature shrub.

[SANBI PlantZAfrica](https://pza.sanbi.org/protea-cynaroides)
describes central true flowers surrounded by colorful bracts, thick stems,
large glossy leaves and head diameters 120–300 mm. Soft pink/silvery bracts are
one documented color form.
[Kew's Protea genus account](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:331879-2/general-information)
supports overlapping series of bracts, dense heads and terminal pollen presenters.
Genus-level reproductive architecture does not establish every species-specific
dimension or hair distribution; those remain explicit evidence gaps.

Author thirty-two substantial overlapping bracts and 128 sampled true florets
with separate perianth and style/pollen-presenter geometry. These are authored
sampling counts, not a claim that natural heads contain exactly 128 flowers.
Florets use real instanced geometry; do not replace the center with beads,
flat fur or a sunflower seed disk. Bract rims yield around firm attachments,
while the stem/head respond more slowly than the fine central organs. Use pale
pink/cream pigment, restrained sheen, granular tissue detail and green glossy
foliage. The implemented fine surface fibers use authored striation and sheen;
no species-specific hair dimensions or borrowed bearded-Protea structures are
claimed. The dense center consists of perianths and styles. Scale, age
offsets, shell thickness and mechanical response are authored. Fire ecology,
nectar, seed production and calibrated optics are outside the simulation.
