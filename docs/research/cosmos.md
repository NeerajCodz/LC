# Cosmos — Cosmos bipinnatus

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cosmos-bipinnatus/) distinguishes ray florets surrounding disk flowers and opposite, deeply cut, thread-like foliage. [Flora of North America's genus account](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=108151) identifies the usual eight ray florets as neuter. Its [species account](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=220003373) describes dentate ray tips; [Flora of Taiwan](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=100&taxon_id=220003373) describes numerous yellow disk flowers. Each central unit is a true corolla with organs.

Authored composition: eight magenta neuter rays, 80 real instanced five-lobed bisexual disk flowers and two ranks of eight green involucral bracts on a slender peduncle. Opposite bipinnately divided leaves use connected rachises and sealed filiform segments. The disk/bract counts, scaled dimensions and selected garden form are authored, not species-wide constants.

Surface: magenta vein stripes, delicately notched ray tips, yellow disk tissue and matte fine foliage. The head sways on an anchored stem; rays flutter while central florets follow their carrier. Floret counts, dimensions, colors, delays and compliance are authored. Fruit, pollination success and measured stiffness are not modeled.
