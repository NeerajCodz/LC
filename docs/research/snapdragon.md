# Snapdragon — Antirrhinum majus

Sources reviewed October 6, 2026. Eight complete flowers and four terminal buds
form an authored single-flowered upright raceme, without claiming a cultivar.

[NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/antirrhinum-majus/)
documents the fused bilateral corolla, two lips, invaginated lower palate,
five-lobed calyx and glossy narrow leaves, opposite below and alternate above.
Bees land on the lower lip and force the mouth open.
[Epidermal research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3906960/)
distinguishes two dorsal, two lateral and one ventral corolla lobe above their
joined proximal tube.
[Flora Iberica](https://www.floraiberica.es/floraiberica/texto/imprenta/vol_XIII/entrega-4/Antirrhinum.pdf)
provides the genus/species morphology and proportional reference.

Build a continuous sealed hollow tube with a vaulted upper lip and raised,
bilateral lower palate, rather than an open Foxglove bell. Mouth articulation
is separate from bud maturation: pointer loading or accessible pulse depresses
the lower lip and releases it elastically. Included stamens/style follow the
folded tube and the mouth carrier. Tissue is coral/rose with a warm yellow
palate and fine epidermal relief; buds remain volumetric and closed. Opening
delays, load/return thresholds, cage compliance and pigment fields are authored,
not calibrated bee-force or cell-growth measurements. No insect or nectar agent
is simulated, and reversal is an inspection convention.
