# Lisianthus — Eustoma russellianum

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [University of Texas's anatomical account](https://w3.biosci.utexas.edu/prc/K12/pages/Eustoma%20russellianum.html) describes five twisted-in-bud lobes, a short fused tube, five stamens, a superior ovary, two stigma branches and branching cymes. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/eustoma-grandiflorum/) uses the historical E. grandiflorum name and describes waxy, clasping gray-green leaves. Preserve this nomenclatural distinction.

Authored composition: three lavender single flowers and two buds; five broadly flaring lobes, five narrow calyx teeth and included organs. Opposite clasping foliage has three-to-five principal veins.

Surface: a dark throat, delicate folds and waxy foliar bloom. Buds untwist into cups; stalks and lobes respond independently. Counts, palette, proportions, motion and reverse bloom are authored. Cultivar identity, microscopic nectaries and measured material properties remain gaps.
