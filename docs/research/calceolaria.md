# Yellow Slipperwort — Calceolaria integrifolia

Reviewed 2 November 2026. Taxon: *Calceolaria integrifolia*, Calceolariaceae. [Implementation guide](../specimens/calceolaria.md).

## Evidence and reference proportions

[RHS’s species account](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/2663/calceolaria-integrifolia/details) describes an upright bushy subshrub with dull, veiny 5–8 cm leaves, hairy beneath, and bright yellow flowers about 12 mm wide in corymbs. [Smithsonian’s Calceolariaceae guide](https://www.naturalhistory.si.edu/sites/default/files/media/file/calceolariaceae.pdf) supplies **genus/family-level**, not species-specific, evidence for the bilateral corolla, small upper hood, inflated lower pouch, four sepals and two stamens with separated thecae. Its climbing-species discussion does not make this species a vine.

## Authored study

The [Royal Botanic Garden Edinburgh species photograph](https://chileanendemics.rbge.org.uk/es/taxa/calceolaria-integrifolia-l) was also reviewed for the rounded pouch and restricted entrance. It prompted a further contour correction: the lateral wall now advances toward the narrow neck instead of turning back into the body and producing a cup-like silhouette. This image is a visual reference only; no photographic asset is shipped.

Four mature flowers and two permanently younger buds form a compact display cluster. Each corolla is one continuous hollow shell with a small upper hood and inflated lower lip, narrow mouth, four sepals, two filaments with paired anther chambers, ovary, style and stigma. A narrowed mouth prevents a mug-like outline; the closed state loses most of the pouch inflation while retaining nondegenerate walls. Included organs follow the same opening transition.

Six rugose oblong leaves in three opposite decussate pairs sit on a 1.78-unit shoot. The flower count, larger display proportions, yellow pigment, warmth at the mouth, roughness 0.70 and scattering 0.06 are authored. This is not the large spotted florist hybrid commonly sold under the same common name. No invented polka-dot pattern is applied.

Independent stalk motion carries each pouch, and local deformation uses 864 / 288 contact nodes with fixed basal attachments. Material relief suggests epidermal texture and lower-leaf hairiness without a separate hair simulation.
