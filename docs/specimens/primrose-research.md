# Primrose — Primula vulgaris

Reviewed October 7, 2026. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/primula-vulgaris/)
supports cream/yellow flowers and a wrinkled, toothed basal rosette.
[Primary heterostyly research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6299000/)
places the stigma at the corolla mouth and anthers halfway down in pin flowers;
thrum flowers reverse these heights. [Population research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10078681/)
describes the fused, notched-lobed corolla.

Author five flowers and two buds on separate basal stalks, all pin-form flowers,
with five lobes/anthers and a long included style. Pale yellow, a saturated
throat, relative size, wrinkles and mechanical response are authored. Keep
continuous corollas, folded normals, lobed rim opening and a textured rosette.

Do not combine pin/thrum heights on one flower. Pollen incompatibility,
genetics, biological reopening and measured mechanics are outside this study.
