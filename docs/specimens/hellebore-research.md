# Hellebore — Helleborus orientalis

Reviewed October 7, 2026. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/helleborus-orientalis/)
describes nodding flowers with five showy sepals, tubular nectaries, numerous
stamens and leathery palmately divided leaves. [Oxford's genus account](https://herbaria.plants.ox.ac.uk/bol/plants400/Profiles/GH/Helleborus)
distinguishes persistent sepals from the modified nectar-bearing petals.

The authored plant has three flowers and one bud, ten nectaries, forty stamens
and five carpels per flower, and basal seven-leaflet foliage. Pale ivory/green
sepals with restrained rose mottling, cupping, thickness and stiffness are
authored. Separate nectary mouths and included organs remain enclosed in buds;
pedicels nod while broad sepals yield around pinned insertions.

Garden material is often hybridized/misidentified; this is a species-informed
single form, not every Lenten Rose cultivar. Nectar secretion, aging, measured
tissue optics and calibrated elastic properties are not simulated.
