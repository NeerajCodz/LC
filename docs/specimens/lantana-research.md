# Lantana — Lantana camara

Modeled taxon: _Lantana camara_ L. Research reviewed October 29, 2026.
This is an authored mixed-color flowering shoot, with no identified cultivar.
The model represents selected anatomy, not every member of the horticultural
_L. camara_ complex. Inventory presence does not resolve that complex.

## Source-backed anatomy

[Flora of China, volume 17](https://www.iplant.cn/foc/pdf/Lantana.pdf)
describes four-angled shoots, rough wrinkled ovate leaves, a slender fused
corolla with four or five spreading lobes, four included didynamous stamens,
and a short included style with an oblique subcapitate stigma. Its species
account documents yellow/orange flowers deepening to red after opening and
extensive cultivar color variation. That account calls the capitula terminal;
this model follows the axillary presentation documented by the sources below.

[New South Wales Flora Online, genus Lantana](https://plantnet.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/cgi-bin/NSWfl.pl?lvl=gn&name=Lantana&page=nswfl)
supports pedunculate axillary heads, bracts, a truncate or shallowly toothed
calyx, four or five corolla lobes, and four included stamens in unequal pairs.

[Kew's Lantana account](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A330561-2/general-information)
reproduces flora descriptions of dense axillary heads with individual bracts
and sometimes an involucre. They describe curved tubes, one larger lobe,
paired anther chambers, and a thick oblique stigma. The corolla can change
color after fertilization. These are genus-level sources; their terminology
must not be presented as measurements of this modeled plant.

[Weeds of Australia, Lantana camara](https://keyserver.lucidcentral.org/weeds/data/03080008-0301-4c05-8c0e-0c0f040b0803/media/Html/lantana_camara.htm)
documents square young shoots becoming round woody stems, opposite petiolate
ovate leaves with rugose serrate/crenate blades and hairy undersides, and
axillary heads of numerous small fused tubular flowers. Its warning about
the cultivated hybrid complex and proposed _L. strigocamara_ treatment remains
a taxonomic limitation of ornamental references.

## Authored construction

The specimen has two unequal axillary heads, each with eighteen open florets
and three persistent compact buds. Ten outer pink, five middle orange and
three inner yellow flowers form fixed illustrative age cohorts. The bloom
control changes opening geometry; it does not advance pollination or biological
age, recolor a flower instantaneously, or claim natural reopening.

Each corolla is one sealed periodic thick shell with four unequal rounded
lobes. There are no overlapping petal overlays. Curved narrow tubes contain
four short stamens at two levels, paired anther chambers, a short style and
an oblique thick stigma. A superior ovary, small membranous calyx and narrow
imbricate bract accompany every floret. Closed bud crowns retain real volume.
The two continuous domed receptacles meet the sessile floral bases; peduncles
bridge the head geometry to the rooted shoot at two upper-shoot insertions.
Those insertions approximate the axillary habit; they do not coincide exactly
with the authored leaf nodes.

Eight opposite decussate leaves have independent green pigments, real serrate
margins, rugose relief and petioles. Four-angled young green shoot tissue grades
into rounder brown basal wood. The shoot and leaf attachments follow the shared
clamped bend while head tissues retain their arrangement. All floret nod values
are zero to preserve sessile attachment to the common receptacle. Geometry,
materials and rest buffers are retained and disposed through the shared helpers
or explicit effects; the frame loop allocates no vectors or materials.

The forty-two corollas use 1,680 desktop and 504 constrained cage nodes in total.
The receptacles, calyces and bracts do not add cages. Ambient views opt into
shared surface batching, while all authored florets remain at every quality.
Minimum angular sampling preserves the rounded lobes; leaf axial samples retain
the small serrations in distant views.

## Verification and limits

The focused Lantana specimen and foliage CPU suites check deterministic sealed
surfaces, positive thickness, finite unit normals, matched folded normals,
nondegenerate triangles at bud/half/full bloom, both whole-plant node ceilings,
cohort counts, periodicity, four unequal lobes, actual included-organ buffers,
two stamen heights, petiole endpoints, rough serrate leaves and square shoots.

Model-unit dimensions, floret counts, precise colors, cohort layout, tissue
thickness, opening offsets and mechanical constants are authored. No measured
material constants, spectral reflectance, scan or developmental time series is
adopted. Physical prickles and geometric corolla/leaf trichomes are not resolved;
the leaf material's pubescence is an optical approximation. Fruits are omitted.
Full front/side/45-degree/macro/bud/half/full visual review and browser/device
validation are coordinated separately; CPU checks alone establish neither visual
realism nor a frame-rate claim.
