# Cyclamen — Cyclamen persicum

Sources reviewed October 6, 2026. This study depicts three single-form flowers
and two younger buds on separate basal stalks; it does not represent a named
large-flowered cultivar.

[RHS](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/101163/cyclamen-persicum-persian-cyclamen/details)
describes nodding flowers with five reflexed, twisted corolla lobes.
[Cyclamen Society](https://www.cyclamen.org/plants/species/cyclamen-persicum/)
documents pointed, strongly twisted lobes 20–45 mm long and 7–20 mm wide, darker
corolla mouths and variable green/grey/silver fleshy foliage with purplish
undersides. Leaf and flower stalks emerge directly from the tuber. Unlike most
cyclamens, fruiting stalks bend downward rather than coiling.

Author a continuous basal corolla cup with five swept-back lobes, included
reproductive organs, curved necks, closed younger buds and sealed silver-zoned
leaf blades. Stalk attachments begin at the basal crown, not a central woody
shoot. Each flower nods independently; free corolla margins have restrained
contact response. Use pale rose/ivory limbs and magenta mouths, with fine satin
microdetail and darker leaf undersides. Counts, model-unit proportions, silver
patterns, response coefficients and inspection timing are authored. Fruit,
seasonal dormancy and measured optics are outside the study; reverse UI bloom
does not imply biological reopening.
