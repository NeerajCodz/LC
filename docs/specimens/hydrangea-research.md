# Lacecap Hydrangea — Hydrangea macrophylla

Sources reviewed October 6, 2026. Select a single lacecap head so the two flower
types remain explicit. Do not substitute an ornamental mophead or a named cultivar.

[UMass Extension](https://www.umass.edu/agriculture-food-environment/landscape/fact-sheets/growing-hydrangeas)
describes flat lacecaps with small fertile flowers inside a showy sterile ring.
[NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/hydrangea-macrophylla/)
provides habit, leaf and flower comparisons.
[Flora of Pakistan's Hydrangea hortensia account](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=5&taxon_id=242325683)
uses the historical name and documents 4–5 showy sepals, five fertile petals,
ten stamens and 3–4 styles. Taxonomic matching uses accepted H. macrophylla in
the pinned WFO inventory, not that historical account's label.

Author ten marginal flowers with four broad petaloid sepals each, and forty-eight
central fertile florets with five small petals, ten stamens and three styles.
Connected branching pedicels support both groups. Showy sepals receive restrained
surface contact; tiny central florets share instanced geometry and carrier motion.
Preserve opposite serrated broad leaves, prominent veins, woody shoot detail,
blue/lavender variation and pale centers. Color is an authored palette, not an
instant soil-pH simulation: the extension account relates color to aluminum
availability and pigments. Counts, proportions, head loading, age distribution
and mechanics are authored; reproductive success and chemical color changes
remain outside the study.
