## Source-backed traits

[Kew accepts this species](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/760275-1)
and recognizes several subordinate taxa. One ornamental study cannot represent
their full variation.

[Flora of China](https://www.iplant.cn/foc/pdf/Pentas.pdf) describes hairy,
quadrangular young branches, opposite petiolate leaves, conspicuous secondary
veins, bristled interpetiolar stipules and congested terminal cymes. Flowers are
sessile or subsessile and distylous. Calyx lobes are hairy and markedly unequal.
The salverform corolla has a slender 17–20 mm tube, a densely bearded throat and
normally five lobes, valvate in bud. In the cultivated long-styled form, the
throat is abruptly swollen around included stamens inserted just below it;
the two linear stigma branches are exserted. Short-styled forms reverse the
relative exposure of stamens and stigma. Cultivated plants are commonly
long-styled and often do not set fruit.

[Leon Levy Native Plant Preserve](https://levypreserve.org/plant-listings/pentas-lanceolata/)
independently documents terminal cymes, five partially fused corolla parts,
five corolla-attached stamens, pubescent opposite leaves and wire-like
interpetiolar stipules. Use five stamens for this selected form.
