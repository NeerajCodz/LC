# Lupine — Lupinus polyphyllus

Sources reviewed October 13, 2026. Depict a blue-violet, species-oriented leafy
shoot rather than identifying a multicolored Russell hybrid as pure
_Lupinus polyphyllus_ Lindl. The pinned inventory candidate is
`wfo-0000213847`; a species mapping does not resolve every subspecies or variety.

[University of Washington Burke Herbarium's FNA species treatment](https://burkeherbarium.org/imagecollection/taxon.php?Taxon=Lupinus+polyphyllus)
documents stout, usually hollow, erect stems; basal and stem leaves with basal
petioles longer than upper ones; palmately compound foliage with
(5 or)6–12(–17) leaflets; approximately whorled flowers on short pedicels in a
6–40 cm raceme. Flowers are 9–15 mm, with a white/yellow banner patch and an
upcurved keel. Flower bracts are deciduous. These are reference ranges, not
model-unit dimensions or a prescription for identical foliage at every node.
[Burke's genus treatment](https://burkeherbarium.org/imagecollection/taxon.php?Taxon=Lupinus)
provides a grooved banner with reflexed sides, two wings, paired keel petals,
ten monadelphous stamens with alternating anther/filament lengths, and a brushy
style. [Kew's Flora Zambesiaca account](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:60436956-2/general-information)
independently supports the closed staminal tube and beaked enclosing keel.
Genus evidence guides the included organs; it does not measure this specimen's
stigma hair lengths or per-flower mechanical response.

[The USDA plant guide](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/plantmaterials/orpmcpg11723.pdf)
warns that commercially named bigleaf lupines can be crosses with other species.
The [Biological Flora review](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ppees.2022.125715)
attributes repeatable keel/style pollen pumping to Haynes and Mesler's study,
and reports a pollen reward without nectar. This is review-level behavioral
evidence, not a measured spring law. Do not add a nectar gland, explosive hatch,
or pollination-driven color transition on that basis.

Planned authored composition: twenty-four complete flowers in four loosely
whorled levels and three younger terminal buds. Every flower retains one banner,
two wings, two enclosing keel halves, a two-lipped calyx, ten joined stamens and
one curved style. Share sealed flower prototypes through real geometry
instancing; retain the internal column rather than replacing it with a bead.
Five leaf groups may use eleven separately sealed leaflets around each shared
petiole junction, with shorter petioles above. Raceme count, whorl spacing,
leaf sampling, pigment, thickness, growth stages and elastic response are
authored. The enclosed organs require matching folded positions and normals.
Reversible opening and any gentle wing/keel depression are inspection
conventions, not a pollinator, fertility or calibrated pollen-release model.
