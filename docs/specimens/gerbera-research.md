# Gerbera — Gerbera jamesonii

Reviewed October 7, 2026. [SANBI's species description](https://biodiversityadvisor.sanbi.org/search/detail/afac1092-f841-4455-af69-17853471ee13)
distinguishes long outer rays, shorter inner rays, disk florets and pinnatifid
basal leaves. [Flora of China's genus treatment](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=2&taxon_id=113481)
describes female bilateral ray florets and bisexual bilateral central florets,
with a three-toothed outer lip and paired inner lobes.

Author thirty-two outer rays, twenty-four inner rays and sixty-four central
florets, including styles and central anthers. Use real sealed bilateral
prototypes, an involucre, leafless scape and lobed basal leaves. Orange-red,
subtler central pigments, counts, age offsets and ray mechanics are authored.

The pinned inventory has two exact-name candidates; preserve this taxonomic
ambiguity rather than claim scientific validation. Cultivar coverage, calibrated
mechanics and collision of every fine floret are outside this study.
