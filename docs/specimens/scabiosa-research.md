# Pincushion Flower — Scabiosa columbaria

Research reviewed 12 October 2026. This dossier guides a dedicated authored 3D
study; inventory mapping and visual plausibility do not establish scientific validation.

## Source-backed traits

Bilateral five-lobed fused flowers have four stamens, an inferior ovary and a lobed style. Leaves are opposite, toothed or divided; outer flowers enlarge the head's margin.

[Primary reference](https://gobotany.nativeplanttrust.org/species/scabiosa/columbaria/).

## Authored implementation

Sixteen enlarged asymmetric marginal florets and twenty-five smaller central florets, with four protruding stamens each, five calyx bristles and green involucral bracts.

Foliage: Opposite divided upper leaves and wider basal blades.

Behavior: Marginal florets deform independently around pinned tubes; protruding organs follow their florets. Bloom can be reversed for inspection; this is not a
claim of biological reopening. Contact, stiffness, damping, colors, counts of
flowers/leaves, dimensions and stage timing are authored. Soft tissue uses sealed
parametric shells with separate folded positions/normals; fine reproductive organs
retain real geometry. Shared atlas sampling supplies cell/pigment irregularity,
with restrained scatter and distinct tissue roughness. Focused/selected physics
uses the existing 2,048/512-node budget; distant views remain ambient.

## Evidence gaps

The Go Botany epicalyx field conflicts with some genus accounts; no claim of verified fruit anatomy is made. Floret counts, head dimensions and mechanical response are authored. No measured elastic constants, spectral pigment data or tissue scans are
available. Full seasonal growth, pollinator action and fertilization are outside
this study. Review front, side, 45 degrees, macro, closed bud and half bloom before
calling the specimen finished.
