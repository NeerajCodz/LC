# Freesia — Freesia refracta

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [Flora of New Zealand](https://www.nzflora.info/factsheet/Taxon/Freesia-refracta.html) describes a wiry stem, stiff leaf fan, right-angled spike, two to seven fleshy flowers, a narrow tube abruptly widening to a funnel, six lobes and three deeply bifid style branches. Its wild flowers are cream/green/yellow; multicolored cultivars should not be silently substituted.

Authored composition: five cream flowers plus two younger buds on a bent unilateral axis, with a yellow lower guide, three stamens, paired spathe valves and three bifid style branches. Sword-shaped basal foliage remains separate from the floral axis.

Surface: subtle fleshy sheen, cream rims, yellow guide and longitudinal leaf veins. Proximal flowers open before terminal buds; independent nodding is anchored to the spike. Counts, dimensions, delays and mechanics are authored. Scent, nectar secretion and calibrated pollination mechanics are outside scope.
