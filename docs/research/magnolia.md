# Magnolia — Magnolia grandiflora

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [Flora of North America through UF's herbarium](https://biogator.org/taxa/index.php?clid=101&taxauthid=1&tid=4931) describes leathery protective bracts and numerous stamens/pistils. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/magnolia-grandiflora/) records large cream flowers, leathery glossy leaves and rusty felted undersides. Genus-level [FNA](https://efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=119452) places stamens on an elongate torus.

Authored composition: nine substantial tepals surrounding a closed elongated receptacle, 179 real instanced stamens and 60 carpels. Two protective bracts and a stout woody shoot connect the solitary head; broad leaves expose contrasting rusty undersides. Counts are an authored selection within the cited anatomical ranges, not measurements of a particular flower.

Surface: ivory wax, fine tepal veins, glossy upper foliage and rougher brown undersides. Massive tepals open slowly and resist high-frequency flutter. Counts and scaled dimensions are authored. Female/male anthesis phases, scent and xylem hydraulics are not modeled; UI reversal is not biological reopening.
