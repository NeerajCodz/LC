# Camellia — Camellia japonica

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [Flora of China, species 84](https://www.iplant.cn/foc/pdf/Camellia.pdf) describes six or seven petals (more in cultivars), about nine bracteoles/sepals, basally fused inner petals, a fused outer filament tube, a superior ovary and three-lobed style. Leathery serrulate leaves and short woody floral attachments are diagnostic.

Authored composition: one rose-pink single flower and a side bud, seven petals, nine overlapping protective organs, numerous yellow anthers on a real basal filament collar, and three style tips. A woody shoot carries alternate glossy leaves.

Surface: substantial satin petals, restrained gloss, fine serrations and pale leaf undersides. Opening preserves fused insertions while distal petals yield. Counts, proportions, palette and response constants are authored. This is not a double cultivar; measured fusion dimensions, pollination and fruit are not simulated.
