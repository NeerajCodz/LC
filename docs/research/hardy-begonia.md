# Hardy Begonia — Begonia grandis

Sources reviewed October 6, 2026. Select the stout, red-veined foliage habit
of subsp. grandis while retaining the species-level catalog/taxonomy name.

[Flora of China](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=2&taxon_id=250073329)
distinguishes four staminate tepals (two broad outer and two smaller inner),
three pistillate tepals, numerous stamens, bifid/U-shaped stigmas and an unequally
three-winged capsule. Female and male flowers must not share a generic five-petal
model. Leaves are asymmetric, obliquely cordate and irregularly serrulate; the
subspecies account describes red undersides or red lower veins.
[NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/begonia-grandis/)
shows pendant dichotomous cymes and red-veined alternate foliage.

Author two male and two female mature flowers with two younger buds on connected
forking pedicels. Male flowers sample forty real stamens (within the documented
28–140 range); females expose their own stigmas and distinct winged ovary.
Use soft pink tissue, gold male organs, asymmetric broad green blades and red
undersides. Petiole, pedicel and tepal responses remain distinct and rooted.
Shell dimensions, flower/organ sampling, age offsets, optics and mechanics are
authored. Ovary-wing proportions need comparison with floral images; mature
capsule dimensions do not establish exact dimensions of a flowering ovary.
Fertility, fruit development and cultivar-wide variation are not simulated.
