# Impatiens — Impatiens walleriana Hook.f.

Research reviewed 13 October 2026. Choose a single-flowered species study rather
than a double cultivar or a New Guinea hybrid. Inventory mapping is not validation.

## Source-backed traits

[Kew's Flora of Tropical East Africa treatment](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:374419-1/general-information)
describes succulent branched shoots, spirally arranged petiolate leaves with
crenate/denticulate margins and petiole nectaries, and few-flowered axillary
racemes. Two small lateral sepals accompany a shallow boat-shaped lower sepal
that narrows abruptly into a long curved slender spur. The dorsal petal is broad
and notched, with a narrow dorsal crest; four lateral petals are united into two
deeply divided pairs. The spur is sepal tissue, not an extra corolla petal.

[PlantNET, Flora of New South Wales](https://plantnet.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/cgi-bin/NSWfl.pl?lvl=sp&name=Impatiens~walleriana&page=nswfl)
independently supports smooth succulent stems, toothed ovate leaves, two or three
flowers together, the long spurred lowermost sepal and fused lateral petal bases.

[Kew's Balsaminaceae flora treatment](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:77126612-1/general-information)
supplies genus/family-level reproductive detail: five short stamens unite into a
cap over the superior ovary and stigma, later falling away as one piece. Mature
flowers are resupinate; the dorsal petal appears above the spurred lower sepal.
Fruit dehiscence concerns capsules, not touch-triggered flower snapping.

## Authored implementation

Three complete flowers and two younger buds on a fleshy branching shoot. Preserve
one dorsal petal and two continuous deeply two-lobed lateral blades, a hollow
lower sepal joined continuously to its long curved spur, two small lateral
sepals, a five-part anther cap and superior ovary. No detached five-petal radial
whorl is substituted for the joined lateral pairs. Alternate toothed leaf blades
and three authored gland samples follow the moving shoot. Current gland placement
uses stem-node samples; distinct petioles and gland insertions sampled from
petiole tissue remain a foliage-anatomy limitation.

Select a coral pink study with a pale center; counts, spacing, model-unit
dimensions, pigment values, translucency, vein detail and mechanical constants
are authored. Succulent shoot displacement is distinct from thinner lateral
petal response; the sepal spur stays attached at every bloom/contact stage.
Bud folds retain sealed thickness and nondegenerate normals. Focused/selected
contact uses the existing 2,048/512 node caps; previews and full garden are ambient.

The dorsal blade extends toward local negative Z so the authored forward-facing
cluster rotations place it above the lower sepal in the assembled flower. Its
insertion stays fixed; the lower sepal/spur, joined lateral pairs, reproductive
organs and cluster poses are unchanged. A regression uses the generated shells
at all five qualities, the actual overview batch vertex path, bloom timing and a
transformed parent to check this dorsal/ventral relationship at half and full
bloom. The correction retains the 690 desktop / 350 constrained contact nodes.
Updated visual acceptance remains pending the corresponding seven-view captures.

## Behavior and evidence gaps

Opening trajectories, bloom offsets, elastic recovery and response to pointer
contact are authored inspection behavior. Reverse bloom is not natural reopening.
Do not infer explosive flower motion from capsule dispersal or simulate anther
cap shedding repeatedly. No species-calibrated stiffness, measured spectral
pigments, tissue scan or measured anthesis sequence was obtained. Verify front,
side, 45 degrees, macro, bud, half and full bloom; side views must reveal the
real hollow curved spur and connected paired petals.
