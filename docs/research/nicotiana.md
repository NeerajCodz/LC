# Jasmine tobacco — Nicotiana alata

Evidence reviewed 29 October 2026. This specimen represents the white, long-tubed form of _Nicotiana alata_, not the broader assortment of short-tubed and colored bedding hybrids.

## Realism review — 30 October 2026

Fresh comparison with [NC State's true-species account and photographs](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/nicotiana-alata/), [Native Plant Trust's photographed flowers](https://gobotany.nativeplanttrust.org/species/nicotiana/alata/), and the [Flora of New Zealand description](https://nzflora.info/factsheet/taxon/Nicotiana-alata.html) identified rounded, overly smooth limbs, straight evenly inclined tubes, regularly spaced pedicels and undersized lower foliage in the previous inspection boards. NC State distinguishes the tall, pointed-flowered species from compact nursery hybrids. NZ Flora describes a lax few-flowered false raceme, a 7–9 cm corolla tube, 1.5–2 cm lobes and lower leaves around 20 × 8 cm, while cultivated leaves may be larger. Its lobes are described as not reflexed, ranging from obtuse to acute; a few photographed curled edges do not justify reversing every lobe.

The refined continuous corolla keeps its long narrow tube, with a gentle downward bow and a more angular five-pointed limb. Shallow radial folds and small edge curls have real depth; the lobes continue to spread rather than folding backward. Unequal pedicel reaches and a mix of inclined mature flowers and more erect terminal buds break the repeated spoke arrangement. Included filaments, anthers, style and stigma follow the same bowed axis and fit inside the actual tube cavity. The five purple anthers and shorter fifth filament remain. Lower spatulate foliage is larger than the mature flower tube and grades into much smaller sessile upper leaves; decurrent wings retain their independent unpitched attachment. Counts, exact sizes, curvature, inclination and developmental poses remain authored choices, not measurements of one living accession.

The modest folded-limb twist is limited so linear interpolation cannot collapse the offset skins while the angular limb opens. New regressions inspect actual bowed tube and creased limb buffers, graded flower poses, uneven connected pedicels, lower-to-upper foliage proportion and real reproductive meshes against the inner corolla wall at bud, half and full bloom. All five quality tiers retain matching finite normals, sealed walls, positive interpolated skin separation and nondegenerate triangles. Counts, contact-cage resolution and overview tessellation are unchanged. Final visual and framing assessment remains part of the coordinated browser review.

## Botanical evidence

[Kew Plants of the World Online, Flora of Tropical East Africa description](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:816845-1/general-information) supports erect glandular-hairy stems, undulating leaves with decurrent winged petioles, and a loose, few-flowered terminal raceme. Flowers have five unequal pointed calyx lobes and a long narrow tube that expands into a white or pale-green salverform corolla. The anthers are purple and globose; four filaments are similar in length while the fifth is shorter. The outside is viscid-pubescent and the inside glabrous. This source also supports a superior ovary, long style and compact stigma. These traits distinguish the specimen from a generic white five-petalled flower.

[Flora of New Zealand](https://nzflora.info/factsheet/taxon/Nicotiana-alata.html) independently describes clasping cauline leaves continuing down the stem, glandular calyces, a pale-green corolla exterior, white interior, and an exceptionally long, gradually widening tube. Its description of equal calyx teeth differs from the unequal-lobed FTEA account; the model follows the latter for slight calyx asymmetry and does not imply universal constancy.

[Flora of North America, Nicotiana genus account](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=122284) supports five stamens, occasional differences in their insertion and length, a two-carpellate ovary and a capitate or slightly divided stigma.

## Construction and authored interpretation

A single loose axis carries seven flowers, five mature and two narrow terminal buds. Their count, insertion spacing, pedicel lengths, orientation, flowering order, head framing and exact dimensions are authored choices. Every pedicel reaches its actual cluster origin; the identity head cluster carries the positive raceme once while the stem below it follows the rooted deformation curve.

One continuous, periodic, double-skinned shell includes the narrow cylinder, widening throat and five spreading lobes. The lobes are part of the wall, never flat petals placed over the mouth. Folded geometry draws the limb into a slightly twisted, narrow bud while retaining its normals and positive thickness. Five pointed, slightly unequal calyx lobes share a separate basal shell. Five real filaments terminate in purple anthers inside the tube, with the fifth visibly shorter. A compact superior ovary and style with capitate stigma complete the reproductive structures. Organ sizes and packing are authored approximations, not measured developmental reconstructions.

Alternate broad leaves have softly undulating entire margins and finite-width bases. Separate sealed decurrent strips are attached to unpitched stem-node frames so they continue down the shoot while each blade inclines away. The shoot also carries shallow longitudinal wings. Pinnate vein shading, a paler underside and a pubescence shader response characterize the glandular foliage. The pale-green tube pigment is authored. Exterior corolla hairs and individually headed glandular hairs are currently omitted; the smooth corolla wall does not reproduce the source-described exterior indumentum.
