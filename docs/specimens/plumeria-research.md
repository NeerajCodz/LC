# Plumeria — Plumeria rubra

Sources reviewed October 4, 2026. This is a flowering shoot with three blossoms
and younger buds, not a reconstruction of a mature tree or a named cultivar.

[Wisconsin Extension](https://hort.extension.wisc.edu/articles/plumeria/) shows
furled buds opening into five overlapping, waxy corolla lobes above a tubular
base, terminal flower clusters, succulent grey-barked branches and pointed,
leathery leaves in P. rubra.
[Kew's Flora of Oman account](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A81275-1/general-information)
supports alternate pointed leaves and white/pink flowers with yellow throats.

Build a continuous fused basal tube joined visually to five overlapping lobes;
retain the contorted bud and concealed stamens/style. Ivory lobes, warm yellow
throats and pink margins are an authored supported color combination. Surface
detail is fine and waxy rather than lacquered. Branches, short pedicels and
thicker lobes have slower responses than papery bracts; leaves move independently.
The detailed lobe cage uses authored stretch compliance `0.000025` and curved
rest-shape compliance `0.00008`. These visual response parameters are not measured
plant tissue constants. The stronger curvature response prevents fleshy limbs
from behaving like folded cloth under overlapping contacts.
The opening slider is an artistic reversible inspection. Fragrance, seasonal
leaf loss, fruit production and calibrated tissue optics are outside this study.
