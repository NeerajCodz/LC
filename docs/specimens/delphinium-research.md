# Delphinium — Delphinium elatum

Reviewed October 7, 2026. [Flora of North America](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=200007828)
describes spurred sepals, elevated lower petal blades with pale hairs, and
divided leaves. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/delphinium-elatum/)
supports blue racemes and lobed foliage; its horticultural counts vary.

Author nine flowers and three buds, five petaloid sepals, a hollow dorsal spur,
four inner petals, twenty stamens and three carpels per flower. Two lower petals
have restrained modeled beards; nectar-bearing upper petals enter the spur.
Deeply divided continuous palmate leaves must not become compound leaflets.
Blue/ivory pigments, flower count, spur angle, proportions and motion are authored.

Cultivated hybrids vary. Hair dimensions, nectar, pollinator loading, measured
mechanics and biological reopening remain evidence gaps, not physical predictions.
