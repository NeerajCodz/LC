# Lobelia — Lobelia erinus

Modeled taxon: _Lobelia erinus_ L. Research reviewed October 13, 2026.
The exact species is `wfo-0000815543` in the pinned inventory. Plan an unnamed
blue-flowered study with a loose raceme; do not identify a horticultural cultivar.
Implementation and visual verification remain pending.

## Source-backed traits

[Kew accepts the species](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A143240-1/general-information).
Its reproduced African flora descriptions record a corolla split dorsally nearly
to its base, two mouth bumps, a small anther tube, and hairy tips with translucent
appendages on the two lower anthers. Some reproduced descriptions retain the
historical synonym _Lobelia senegalensis_; keep that provenance explicit.

[Flora of New Zealand](https://floraseries.landcareresearch.co.nz/taxa/abdbb63b-8040-4d41-aff4-a2e8c67dbf7b)
describes slender shoots that may trail without rooting at nodes, broader
elliptic/obovate/spathulate toothed lower leaves and narrower usually entire
leaves below the inflorescence. Loose racemes have long slender pedicels and
narrow calyx lobes. The three broad lower corolla lobes slope downwards and
usually have pale throat bands; the lower two blue anthers are bearded.

[SANBI's botanical account](https://pza.sanbi.org/lobelia) supplies genus-level
evidence for alternate leaves without stipules, two smaller erect upper lobes,
three spreading lower lobes, partially fused filaments and anthers fused into
a tube around the style. Its protandry description has the style pushing pollen
through the anther tube before the stigma lobes separate. This reproductive
sequence is genus-level evidence, not a measured timing study of this specimen.

## Authored construction and behavior

Build a dedicated dorsally split corolla: two narrow upper lobes and three broader
fan lobes, connected proximal tissue, mouth bumps, a projecting anther tube around
the style, two bearded anther tips and five slender calyx lobes. Preserve loose
raceme spacing, long pedicels, alternate foliage and different lower and upper
leaf shapes. Do not substitute five radial petals or five independent elongated
stamens. A blue form with pale throat guides is an authored selection, not a
cultivar identification.

The dorsal cleft is a real anatomical opening. Its inner and outer surfaces need
positive thickness and sealed cut walls along both margins. Closed topology of
the tissue must not bridge or fill the natural opening. Bud tissue may tuck and
overlap for inspection, with matched folded positions and normals; its detailed
folding trajectory remains authored until supported by stronger bud evidence.

Slender-pedicel nodding and low-shoot bending are authored growth-habit
interpretations. Keep each pedicel insertion attached to the shared rooted bend.
Pollen presentation is not repeated elastic pumping. Reverse bloom is an
inspection convention, not a claim that mature flowers naturally close and
reopen. Exact flower counts, raceme dimensions, wall thickness, palette fields,
hair density, stage offsets and all mechanical coefficients are authored.
Preserve the existing focused contact budgets, atlas reuse, reduced motion and
sharp macro rendering.

## Evidence gaps and verification

No measured elastic constants, spectral pigments, scan, species-specific opening
time course or calibrated pollen-presentation timing has been adopted. Wild,
naturalized and cultivated forms vary in stature, foliage and coloration. The
genus-level anther/style account must remain distinguished from species-level
flora observations.

Verify five unequal corolla lobes, the uninterrupted dorsal cleft, sealed cut
walls, included/protruding organ placement, the fused anther tube, bearded lower
tips, narrow calyx lobes, long attached pedicels and alternate foliage in front,
side, 45-degree, macro, bud, half-bloom and full-bloom views. This dossier claims
no implementation tests or visual checks.
