# Zinnia — Zinnia elegans

Reviewed October 7, 2026. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/zinnia-elegans/)
describes variable semi-double/double heads, overlapping involucral bracts and
opposite sessile, clasping, rough leaves with basal veins. [Flora of North America](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=250067848)
lists Z. elegans under its historical Z. violacea treatment; its indexed entry
was available but the full page timed out. Individual cultivar counts differ
substantially, and this treatment is not an exact-name taxonomy validation.

Author one semi-double head with twenty-eight magenta rays and thirty-two golden
disk florets, each a five-lobed tube with included anther tube and paired style
arms. Keep layered ligules, an involucre and opposite attached leaves. Five
basal veins, roughness, pubescence, proportions and mechanical response are authored.

Two exact-name candidates occur in the pinned inventory; retain that ambiguity.
Dense florets retain their counts on mobile without individual cages. Seed
production, calibrated optical/elastic properties and cultivar-wide realism
are not claimed.
