# African Violet — Streptocarpus ionanthus

Research reviewed 12 October 2026. This dossier guides a dedicated authored 3D
study; inventory mapping and visual plausibility do not establish scientific validation.

## Source-backed traits

Short tubular violet flowers above a basal rosette of thick, hairy leaves; compound dichasial inflorescence. The older name is Saintpaulia ionantha.

[Primary reference](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/streptocarpus-ionanthus/)
[Additional reference](https://naturalhistory.si.edu/sites/default/files/media/file/3ornamental-garden-plants-guianaspart-2.pdf).

## Authored implementation

Five open single-form flowers and one bud, each with a short fused five-lobed bilateral corolla and two yellow connivent anthers, a style and five sepals.

Foliage: Scalloped succulent blades, raised veins and quality-scaled whitish hairs.

Behavior: Restrained corolla rim flex, independently moving flower stalks and low rosette response. Bloom can be reversed for inspection; this is not a
claim of biological reopening. Contact, stiffness, damping, colors, counts of
flowers/leaves, dimensions and stage timing are authored. Soft tissue uses sealed
parametric shells with separate folded positions/normals; fine reproductive organs
retain real geometry. Shared atlas sampling supplies cell/pigment irregularity,
with restrained scatter and distinct tissue roughness. Focused/selected physics
uses the existing 2,048/512-node budget; distant views remain ambient.

## Evidence gaps

The Smithsonian Guianas treatment under Saintpaulia supplies two stamens and joined anthers; cultivar flower shapes vary and this single form is authored. No measured elastic constants, spectral pigment data or tissue scans are
available. Full seasonal growth, pollinator action and fertilization are outside
this study. Review front, side, 45 degrees, macro, closed bud and half bloom before
calling the specimen finished.
