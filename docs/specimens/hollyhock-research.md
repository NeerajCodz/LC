# Single Hollyhock — Alcea rosea

Sources reviewed October 13, 2026. Depict the single five-petalled form of
_Alcea rosea_ L., with a visible staminal column. _Althaea rosea_ (L.) Cav. is a
historical synonym in [Flora of New Zealand](https://www.nzflora.info/factsheet/taxon/Alcea-rosea.html).
The pinned inventory candidate is `wfo-0000525183`; no named cultivar is claimed.

[Flora of China](https://www.iplant.cn/foc/pdf/Alcea.pdf)
describes very short pedicels, leafy bracts and axillary solitary/fascicled
flowers collected into a tall spikelike inflorescence. A cup-shaped, usually
6–7-lobed epicalyx surrounds a separate five-lobed bell-shaped calyx. Broad
obovate-triangular petals have narrowed hairy claws and notched tips; reference
corolla diameter is 6–10 cm and petal length about 4 cm. The glabrous staminal
column is about 2 cm, with short free filaments and compact yellow anthers
toward its apex; style branches are numerous and hairy, with filiform stigmas.
Leaves are nearly orbicular, palmately 5–7-lobed and stellate-hairy.
[NC State Extension](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/alcea-rosea/)
confirms five petals and five sepals, alternate leaves that become smaller
upward, wrinkled venation, a hairy lower surface, and a mostly unbranched
flowering stem. Neither account establishes one invariant anther count.

Authored composition: five complete rose-pink flowers and two
younger upper buds on one leafy shoot. Each open flower has five substantial,
overlapping, cupped, notched petals, a separate seven-lobed epicalyx and
five-lobed calyx. Use a continuous central staminal column with thirty-two
sampled short filament/anther units and sixteen slender style branches; those
sampling counts are artistic choices. Do not reuse Hibiscus's five round stigma
tips or add the dense petaloid center of a double cultivar. Broad, shallowly
lobed continuous leaf blades retain palmate veins and quality-scaled stellate
pubescence, rather than becoming compound leaflets.

Short axillary flower attachments and leaf petioles must follow the rooted
shoot, meeting their actual insertion points. Folded columns, anthers, styles,
calyx and petal claws require coherent bud placement and normals. Petal
unfurling, staggered developmental stages, exact pigment, shell thickness,
hair sampling, forces, stiffness and damping are authored. These references
do not supply a time-resolved anthesis trajectory or measured loading law;
the reversible bloom control is an inspection convention. No pollinator,
reproduction or nectar transport is simulated.

The authored petal claw has a 0.032-unit half-width, tapering into the same 0.314-unit maximum blade width. This finite insertion keeps nonadjacent cage warp roots beyond the 0.013-unit contact thickness at the tested floral scale; a string-thin insertion trapped the free blade in residual self-contact after a pulse. These dimensions are modeling choices, not measured botanical proportions.
