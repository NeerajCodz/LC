# Carnation — Dianthus caryophyllus

Sources reviewed October 4, 2026. The study represents a pink cultivated double
form, not the five-petalled wild flower or a named cultivar.

The Australian [OGTR biology dossier](https://www.ogtr.gov.au/sites/default/files/files/2021-07/the_biology_of_carnation_-_v2.1_february_2020.pdf)
describes clawed petals, ten stamens and two styles in wild forms, and cultivated
double forms with up to forty petals. A cylindrical, toothed calyx and basal
bracts surround the petal claws. Opposite linear leaves sheath conspicuous stem
nodes. Reproductive structures can be enclosed or converted to petals in doubles.
[NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/dianthus-caryophyllus/) provides
habit and photographic comparisons of double flowers and jointed shoots.

[Opening research](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2877890/) distinguishes
emergence, elongation, expansion and outward bending of outer petals. The model
uses those stages to guide the reversible inspection control, without modeling
cell growth or predicting anthesis timing.

Author forty individually varied, deeply folded, fringed petals with narrow claws;
do not reuse the rose's spiral silhouette. Pale pink tissue and deeper edge/vein
pigment remain matte, with subtle papillae and restrained back scattering.
Jointed stems bend at a fixed base; free petal margins yield under wind and contact.
Thickness, pigment spectra, compliance, damping and contact forces are authored.
Pollen stays on sheltered organs rather than bursting into the air on a tap.

Authored proportions use model units, not millimetres: the four ten-petal
layers have nominal blade lengths 0.94, 0.80, 0.66 and 0.52, with 0.009 shell
thickness. The calyx is 0.29 long with basal radius 0.135; four bracts wrap its
base. Petal claws insert within the calyx, narrow when folded, and spread by
layer while the inner blades arch over the center. This is a selected double
form with ten sheltered stamens and two styles; the degree of reproductive-organ
conversion and fertility of a particular cultivar has not been established.
