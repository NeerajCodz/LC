# Bougainvillea — Bougainvillea glabra

Sources reviewed October 4, 2026. This study depicts a thorny flowering shoot
with three cymes, each containing three bracts and three true flowers.

[Flora of China](https://www.iplant.cn/foc/pdf/Bougainvillea.pdf) documents
three-flowered cymes, each flower attached along its bract midvein, magenta
papery bracts, distinctly angled floral tubes, five terminal lobes and six to
eight included stamens in B. glabra. Leaves are alternate, ovate/lanceolate and
mostly glabrous; axillary thorns and pendant branches characterize the shoot.
[NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/bougainvillea-glabra/) distinguishes
the showy bracts from the small true flowers and the plant's scrambling support.

Make individually curved, thin but sealed bracts with branching venation, a
restrained back-light response and small crease irregularities. True flowers
retain their greenish ribbed tubes and cream mouths, plus sheltered organs.
Magenta is confined to bracts; changing the display color does not recolor the
true flowers or leaves. Midribs remain constrained while free margins flutter
and meet neighboring bracts. Bract expansion and tube-mouth opening use separate
inspection timing. Counts, thickness, pigments and motion constants are authored;
the shoot depicts established support rather than dynamic climbing or seasons.
