# Gladiolus — Gladiolus communis

Reviewed October 7, 2026. [Flora of North America](https://www.efloras.org/florataxon.aspx?flora_id=1&taxon_id=220005610)
describes weakly two-ranked flowers, an oblique fused tube, six unequal tepals
and narrow median white markings on the outer three. [Kew's genus account](https://powo.science.kew.org/taxon/urn%3Alsid%3Aipni.org%3Anames%3A30066908-2/general-information)
supports unilateral stamens and a three-branched style.

Author eight flowers and three terminal buds, reddish purple with white guides,
three stamens per flower and sword-shaped sheathing leaves. Keep a continuous
perianth base beneath the unequal free limbs, bracts, included organs and
lower-to-upper opening. Leaf/stalk bending and softer tepal edges differ.

Counts, relative floral dimensions, age delays and mechanical coefficients are
authored. The specimen does not represent all large garden Gladiolus hybrids;
corm growth, measured stiffness and reproductive success are outside scope.
