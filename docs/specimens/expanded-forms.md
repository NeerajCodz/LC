# Expanded floral forms

Project LC continues toward an open-ended collection of every flower. These specimens add hollow, wrapped, pendant, and asymmetric forms to the existing layered-petal engine. Sources were reviewed September 10, 2026. They guide construction; authored dimensions, bloom timing, and wind constants are not measured biological data.

## Geometry and rendering

`floralSurfaces.ts` samples open and folded mid-surfaces, offsets front/back surfaces along normals, and seals their boundaries. It supplies the corona, spathe, pouch, spur and boat forms. Each carries matched morph positions and normals. `FloralParts` initializes morph targets explicitly and disposes geometry and materials on replacement. `BladePart` supports independently positioned and delayed tepals outside radial whorls.

`floralOrgans.ts` combines tapered curved tubes for stamens, paired anthers and stigma ridges. `spadixGeometry.ts` builds a continuous capped axis with packed florets expressed as geometric relief, avoiding detached bead-like anthers. Its base attaches at the spathe origin, and its upper end rounds off. Calla uses finer relief and a greenish basal zone: [Oxford's account](https://herbaria.plants.ox.ac.uk/bol/plants400/Profiles/yz/Zantedeschia) describes the tiny flowers packed around the column, with female flowers at its base. These zones remain simplified.

Those organs are real geometry; no photograph, sprite, or billboard is used. Counts for dense small organs vary by quality and are artistic sampling choices, not taxonomic constants. Tissue translucency is approximated by the shared PBR shader, not volumetric subsurface scattering. The existing optional vgpu tissue/HDR bakes and complete WebGL fallbacks remain shared.

## Integration and limits

Every specimen appears in canonical `/flower/<slug>/` routes, selectors, gallery, and both garden layouts. Navigation order preserves established entries and appends new specimens. Garden camera distance derives from planted envelopes. Macro geometry remains sharp; no depth-of-field or bloom glow is added. Mobile retains the bounded framebuffer, lower tessellation and floret counts, and capped render cadence.

Non-radial structures can specify a local `headCenter` for specimen, preview and garden macro framing. Portrait garden controls flow below the viewing surface so that they do not cover specimens. The shared garden slider reaches both endpoints for every planting; species timing varies without adding an index-based bloom offset.

The garden is an artistic collection, not a horticulturally compatible planting plan. Leaf and bloom contacts still use conservative envelopes rather than cloth collision. Wind coefficients are authored from growth habit; sources do not supply measured spring constants. Fine hairs, reproductive microanatomy and cultivar-specific variation are simplified. This collection is not a scientific identification reference or a claim of photographic equivalence.

## Verification

Use the seven-view development fixture for front, side, three-quarter, macro, bud, half-open and full bloom. Numerical checks cover finite sealed shells, positive thickness, deterministic generation and matching morph normals. Browser checks cover all new routes, bloom controls, macro mode, garden selection, retained gallery scenes, orbit/zoom and constrained framebuffers. Physical-phone testing remains necessary before making hardware performance claims.
