# Cosmos — Cosmos bipinnatus

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/cosmos-bipinnatus/) distinguishes ray florets surrounding disk flowers and opposite, deeply cut, thread-like foliage. Each yellow central unit must be a true corolla with organs, not a decorative sphere. Ray sex and detailed involucre counts need a flora account before scientific validation.

Authored composition: eight magenta rays, real instanced five-lobed disk florets and two ranks of green involucral bracts on a slender peduncle. Opposite bipinnately divided leaves use connected rachises and narrow sealed segments.

Surface: magenta vein stripes, delicately notched ray tips, yellow disk tissue and matte fine foliage. The head sways on an anchored stem; rays flutter while central florets follow their carrier. Floret counts, dimensions, colors, delays and compliance are authored. Fruit, pollination success and measured stiffness are not modeled.
