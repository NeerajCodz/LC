# English Bluebell — Hyacinthoides non-scripta

Research reviewed 12 October 2026. This dossier guides a dedicated authored 3D
study; inventory mapping and visual plausibility do not establish scientific validation.

## Source-backed traits

Dark blue flowers form a one-sided inflorescence. Unequal staminal filaments carry white pollen, distinguishing English bluebell from the Spanish species.

[Primary reference](https://herbaria.plants.ox.ac.uk/bol/plants400/Profiles/GH/Hyacinthoides).

## Authored implementation

Eight nodding fused six-lobed bells and two terminal buds on a curved raceme; six included stamens per bell and a central style.

Foliage: Narrow basal strap blades with parallel venation.

Behavior: Independently nodding bells, restrained reflexed-tip deformation and anchored scape sway. Bloom can be reversed for inspection; this is not a
claim of biological reopening. Contact, stiffness, damping, colors, counts of
flowers/leaves, dimensions and stage timing are authored. Soft tissue uses sealed
parametric shells with separate folded positions/normals; fine reproductive organs
retain real geometry. Shared atlas sampling supplies cell/pigment irregularity,
with restrained scatter and distinct tissue roughness. Focused/selected physics
uses the existing 2,048/512-node budget; distant views remain ambient.

## Evidence gaps

Bell count, nod angles, leaf dimensions and opening delays are authored; this is not the upright, open-flowered Spanish bluebell. No measured elastic constants, spectral pigment data or tissue scans are
available. Full seasonal growth, pollinator action and fertilization are outside
this study. Review front, side, 45 degrees, macro, closed bud and half bloom before
calling the specimen finished.

## Construction and verification

The dedicated `BLUEBELL_MODEL` uses ten independently posed, continuous periodic
perianths: eight open bells and two narrow terminal buds. Six strongly recurved
tips remain connected to the tube. Every flower retains six included filaments,
two chambers per white anther and a central style; alternating filament heights
are deliberately unequal. The one-sided pedicels end at their exact flower
insertions on the same arched axis. Five sealed strap leaves have parallel
venation, shallow transverse cupping and independently posed drooping ends.

The Oxford account above was re-read on 12 October 2026 and directly supports the
one-sided dark-blue flowers, unequal filaments and white pollen. Small paired
bracts, the modeled ovary proportions and central style length remain authored
interpretations, rather than measurements from that account. There are no
separate decorative green sepals on the fused perianth.

Focused cages measure 720 desktop and 320 constrained nodes. The dedicated unit
checks cover the one-sided downward axes, attached pedicels, organ counts,
six-lobed continuity, bud enclosure, both foliage morph states, sealed edges,
positive thickness, unit normals and nondegenerate full/half/closed triangles.
Browser and seven-view visual inspection are recorded separately after catalog
integration; these unit checks do not establish visual realism.

The terminal buds additionally close with six imbricated sealed tepal-tip blades
per bell. Each grows directly from its periodic wall rim and overlaps across the
axis with finite width, rather than collapsing a ring of vertices to one point.
These tiny tips inherit their flower pose and opening state; they do not receive
separate contact cages, so the node measurements above remain unchanged.
