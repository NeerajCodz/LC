# Anemone — Anemone coronaria

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [NC State](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/anemone-coronaria/) distinguishes colored sepals, a dark center, dissected basal leaves and a leafy whorl below solitary flowers. The account gives seven to nine sepals for its described form.

Authored composition: eight violet petaloid sepals, a compact carpel dome and numerous dark anthers. Separate divided basal foliage and a three-part leafy involucre attach below the bloom. All organs use real geometry; mature seed heads are excluded.

Surface: thin satin sepals, radial dark basal pigmentation and a powdery anther finish. Cupped buds open to a shallow bowl; the anchored peduncle sways more than the central receptacle. Counts, proportions, color, delay and compliant motion are authored; exact cultivar, temperature-driven closure and calibrated stiffness remain gaps.
