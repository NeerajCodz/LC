# Alstroemeria — Alstroemeria aurea

Reviewed October 7, 2026. [Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria](https://hortflora.rbg.vic.gov.au/taxon/ada6ee36-5340-11e7-b82b-005056b0018f)
describes yellow/orange flowers with darker marks on the upper two inner tepals.
[PlantNET's genus account](https://plantnet.rbgsyd.nsw.gov.au/cgi-bin/NSWfl.pl?lvl=gn&name=Alstroemeria&page=nswfl)
supports six unequal free tepals, six stamens, three style branches, an inferior
ovary and alternate resupinate leaves with longitudinal veins.

Author three flowers and two buds on a branched leafy cyme. Upper inner tepals
alone receive the dark streaks/flecks; the outer whorl is broader. Explicit
leaf twisting turns the blade over while maintaining a continuous attachment.
Lengths, orange/yellow shades, opening delays and tissue response are authored.

Genus-level resupination/reproductive traits are labeled accordingly. This is
not a model of all florist hybrids, and leaf handedness, calibrated optics,
mechanics or explosive seed dispersal are not predicted.
