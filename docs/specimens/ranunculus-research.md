# Ranunculus — Ranunculus asiaticus

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [NC State's species account](https://plants.ces.ncsu.edu/plants/ranunculus-asiaticus/) describes cupped flowers, ornamental double forms, five sepals in its illustrated specimen and deeply dissected, alternate foliage. This is a cultivated double study; it does not stand for the wild single form.

Authored composition: one apricot head with 48 overlapping petals, five green sepals and divided foliage. Thin sealed blades nest with distinct inner/outer curvature and delayed opening. Peduncle insertion remains fixed while petals yield independently under detailed contact.

Surface: soft longitudinal veining, pale rims, matte papillae and restrained transmission. Bud layers enclose the center. Counts, geometry dimensions, bloom interpolation, pigment and elasticity are authored. Cultivar identity, measured mechanics, nectar and fruit are unresolved or outside scope.
