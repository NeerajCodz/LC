# Crocus — Crocus vernus

Reviewed 8 October 2026. [Native Plant Trust Go Botany](https://gobotany.nativeplanttrust.org/species/crocus/vernus/) documents two perianth cycles, three stamens, an inferior ovary and a white stripe along narrow leaves. The model must distinguish stamens from the branched stigma and must not use saffron's long red styles for this species.

Authored composition: three violet flowers and a younger bud, six tepals per flower and a continuous basal tube, three yellow anthers and three short orange stigma branches. The inferior ovary remains at the basal insertion; flower stalk/tube and leaves rise separately.

Surface: violet longitudinal tepal veins and clear white foliar midstripes. Thickened tepal bases yield less than their rims. Bloom is reversible inspection, not a temperature-calibrated closing cycle. Counts, size, opening schedule and elasticity are authored; exact cultivar and measured stigma proportions remain gaps.
