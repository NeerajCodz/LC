# Sweet Pea — Lathyrus odoratus

Sources reviewed October 4, 2026. A supported flowering shoot carries three
magenta/violet flowers; colors and arrangement do not assert a named cultivar.

[University of Michigan CLIMBERS](https://climbers.lsa.umich.edu/lathyrus-odoratus/)
describes winged stems, paired leaflets, semi-sagittate stipules and branched leaf
tendrils. Flowers have one banner, two free wings and a paired keel; nine stamens
are united and one is free, surrounding a hairy ovary and style.
[Córdoba floral-mechanics study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3177674/)
includes L. odoratus and describes reversible depression of wings/keel and a
style-borne pollen presenter. This is distinct from explosive legume mechanisms.

Build bilateral folded surfaces and an enclosed keel, with visible claws and
attachments. Give the banner rippling margins, finer satin tissue and asymmetric
veins. Couple wing/keel depression to local pointer force or accessible pulse,
then restore the resting pose. Tendrils depict an established support; they do
not search for or dynamically acquire arbitrary neighbors. The support remains
fixed and the free shoot bends. Forces, compliance and timings are authored, not
values calibrated from the paper; actual insect behavior and reproduction are
not simulated.
